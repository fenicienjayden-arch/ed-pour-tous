1. Mole

1 mole = 6,02 x 10^23 particules
Masse molaire: H=1g/mol, O=16g/mol
H2O = 18 g/mol

2. Concentration

C = n / V (mol/L)
n = nombre moles, V = volume litre
Ex: 2 moles dans 1L = 2 mol/L

3. pH

pH <7 = acide (citron, coca)
pH =7 = neutre (eau)
pH >7 = basique (savon)
